## Synopsis

"**BoJack Horseman** is a Netflix adult animated tragicomedy series that follows a washed-up, self-loathing, alcohol-dependent actor and former sitcom star from the hit 1990s fictional sitcom *Horsin' Around*, who struggles with depression, trauma, and addiction while trying to find meaning and redemption in Hollywoo, a satirical version of Hollywood."

## Main Characters

- :horse: **BoJack Horseman** (voiced by [Will Arnett](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0004715/)) - "A washed-up, self-destructive 90s sitcom star horse battling depression and a cycle of bad choices."

## Why I Like the Series

I like the series *BoJack Horseman* because of how relatable it is and how it is more than just a simple comedic series, as it goes much deeper by exploring serious topics in life, like mental health, relationships, personal struggles, and the regrets and consequences of our actions.
